## ✨ About Me

I am a storyteller at heart, someone who finds meaning in words, images, and the countless ways stories shape our lives. Poetry has been my quiet companion, helping me translate emotions into verses that linger beyond the moment. Storytelling, in all its forms—whether through **animes**, **movies**, **books**, or **conversations**—has profoundly impacted me, teaching me to see the world through many lenses.

My journey through life has brought me closer to **spirituality** and the search for something profound, something that completes me beyond the routines of everyday existence. While I work as an IT professional in the familiar rhythm of a 9‑to‑5, I often feel the pull toward deeper questions and creative pursuits. Perhaps that longing is why I write, paint, and explore new paths like **Vedic astrology**—each one a step toward understanding myself and the universe.

I am still learning, still searching, and still creating. Whether it’s a poem scribbled late at night, a brushstroke on canvas, or a reflection on life’s mysteries, I believe every act of expression is part of my journey. This blog is where I share those fragments—stories, poems, and thoughts—hoping they resonate with others who are also searching for meaning.
