Who am I

April 25, 2026

Childhood and the Weight of Worth Growing up, I was an underconfident kid, always anxious about my worth as a person. I was good at studies, often coming first in class. Mostly because when I came first, people clapped, showered compliments—external validation became my measure of worth. At the same time, I worked hard simply because I couldn’t stand holding back my best effort. Even today, this is a challenge: sometimes I don’t realize how much to put into work. If my superior isn’t satisfied, my anxiety spikes—losing sleep, eating badly, all for work.
